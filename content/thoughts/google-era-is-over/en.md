These past few years, Google has proved one thing: don't let others know your real ability, because you will find out, there isn't much ability.

Thinking back to two years ago, I always believed that whether it was Google's phones or AI, they must be holding back something big.

A company that created DeepMind and Transformer, with an extremely strong software development team, a company so many software engineers dream of.

I always believed Google was very strong. Until now, I can honestly tell everyone: Google's era is over. Like many companies that were once glorious, it will go into history.

Honestly, even now I am still very curious. Those companies that gathered talent from all over the world, yet still cannot stop going into decline.

Strict interviews, only people from the world's top 100 schools have a chance to get in, full of passionate young people and people who write code all gathered together.

How can they keep doing worse and worse?

I don't have the ability to get into these companies. At most, I have only seen what it's like in relatively large software companies in Taiwan. Taiwan's software is unbelievably bad, so there isn't much reference value either.

I wonder if any gods working at Google could talk about it. After all, ordinary people really don't get to meet the god race.
