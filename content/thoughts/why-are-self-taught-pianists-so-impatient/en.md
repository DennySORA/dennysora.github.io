And in this situation it is not reading the score at all but muscle memory, right?

Once they get a new score, don't they have to count there again?

On top of that it really sounds very noisy, all the notes are pressed with full force, the fingers also look very awkward, there is completely no technique to speak of

Later on, basically being able to play when seeing the score, not having to keep looking at the fingers, while also being able to read the score structurally, roughly knowing what chord it is when seeing the structure, and then playing it easily.
